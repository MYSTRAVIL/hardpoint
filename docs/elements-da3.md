# da3 inventory: MONOLITH logistics terminal reel

Source: `in/VID_danneuenhaus_20261106_170942_da3.mp4`, stem `da3`. 1080x608, 24 fps, 879 video frames (36.625 s; the container says 36.75 s because the audio runs slightly longer). Frame n is at n/24 s, so every time below is exact to one frame (42 ms). All px values are at 1080x608. 73 elements, 30 reference frames.

This file is evidence, not a build list. The library takes the reel's style, not its content. [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md#the-style) turns these elements into style rules and lists the story and branding parts to leave out. The `home` column below records the first reading of the reel and is superseded by the brief.

## Method

- Strips viewed: 12 fps for 0 to 3.6 s, 9.9 to 12.5 s and 34.8 to 36.6 s; 24 fps for 3.5 to 4.5, 7.8 to 9.9, 24.3 to 25.3, 30.4 to 31.0 and 33.8 to 34.8, plus 24 fps crops of the header (13.8 to 14.5 and 19.3 to 20.0), the table rows (15.0 to 15.5), the orbit panel (19.9 to 20.5), the lockup (1.7 to 2.3) and the yard counters (30.75 to 31.4); 8 fps for the rest (4.5 to 7.8, 12.5 to 13.8, 14.5 to 19.3, 20.5 to 24.3, 25.3 to 30.4, 31.0 to 33.8), plus full-resolution frames and zoomed crops. Positions, speeds, colours and cell counts were measured with numpy on the decoded frames (bounding boxes per frame, cross-correlation for scroll speed, pixel counts for fills, an affine fit for the photo). Values marked "read" were read by eye from small text.
- Reference frames: `reference/da3/`, 30 JPEGs, 1080x608, `-q:v 3`. Colour gotcha for anyone re-extracting: the source is tagged BT.709 and the plain `-frames:v 1 -q:v 3` command desaturates the orange (measured 231,100,7 instead of the true 244,105,2). These frames were cut with `-vf "select=eq(n\,N),scale=in_color_matrix=bt709:out_color_matrix=bt601:out_range=pc:flags=accurate_rnd+full_chroma_int"`, which lands within 2 levels.
- Fades are almost absent. State changes are hard frames, flickers, pixelation ramps or eased slides. The one fade measured is the grey ground cells decaying over 12.0 to 12.3 s (da3-33).
- The audio track holds a near-constant level (about -12 dB RMS in every second of the reel). It was not analysed for cue sync or content.

## How to read the tables

- `id` is `da3-NN`. Ids 01 to 05 are screen-wide treatments; 06 onward follow first appearance in time.
- `cat`: component, transition, layout, type, data-viz, background.
- `home`: `base` = Svelte component on a headless primitive; `motion` = opt-in Svelte transition or action; `canvas` = Canvas/WebGL component or pass; `pattern` = layout recipe or docs, no component.
- `colour` gives the reel's colour role, then the contract mapping after an arrow. The contract has no orange: every orange role maps to titanium `#E6E3DC` (inverse fill, black text) or to a status hue only where the element means that status. No orange is proposed anywhere.
- Frame links open `reference/da3/tSS.SSs.jpg`.

## Open points

- Typeface names (display, mono, pixel) are not identified; only behaviour was measured.
- The pixel-grid texture on orange (da3-05) may be an overlay or an encode artefact.
- Orbit micro labels (about 5 px) are illegible; body orbit speeds were not measured.
- Counter and total values were read from strips except those visible in saved frames (2436, 3, 3, 1, 0 at 31.50; 4872, 278, 266, 108, 11 at 33.00; 1159 at 17.50).
- The alert strip band geometry in the three frames before it settles (33.88 to 33.96) is approximate.
- Spine wordmark (da3-24): clipped by the panel edge or hidden behind the panel; both look identical.
- The reel never says what the X box and the "!" tile mean (placeholder, error, loading).
- Cell flicker (da3-33) and hot cells (da3-69) look random and the seed cannot be recovered. The stutter reveal (da3-08) is deterministic: both runs follow the same script.
- Easing names (for example "expo in-out") describe per-frame deltas at 24 fps, not recovered curves.
- The timeline dates use two formats (`2237-10-28` and `2237.11.02`); treat as sample data.
- The boot log text (da3-09) was read from a 4x upscaled crop of 6 px type; single letters may be wrong.
- The photo drift steps at about 10 Hz (da3-63) were measured on shots 2 and 3 only; whether the orbit diagram and the other graphics also update at a reduced rate was not measured.
